# Customer Support Policies FAQ

Q: How can I contact TechFlow customer support?
A: TechFlow customer support can be reached by email at support@techflow.example, through live chat on the TechFlow website, or by phone. Which channels you can use depends on your support plan.

Q: What is the TechFlow customer support email address?
A: The TechFlow customer support email address is support@techflow.example. Email is available to every customer on all support plans, including the free Basic plan.

Q: What are the TechFlow customer support hours?
A: TechFlow customer support is available Monday to Friday, 8 am to 8 pm Eastern Time. Support is closed on the 12 company holidays, except for 24/7 urgent P1 phone support on the Premium plan.

Q: Is TechFlow customer support open on weekends?
A: No, standard TechFlow customer support hours are Monday to Friday only, so the team is closed on Saturday and Sunday. Premium plan customers can still call the 24/7 phone line for urgent P1 issues.

Q: Is TechFlow customer support open on holidays?
A: TechFlow customer support is closed on the 12 company holidays. Tickets sent on a holiday are answered on the next business day, and Premium customers can still call for urgent P1 issues at any time.

Q: How many company holidays does TechFlow have?
A: TechFlow has 12 company holidays per year, and customer support is closed on those days. The holiday list is published in the TechFlow help center at help.techflow.example.

Q: What time zone are TechFlow support hours in?
A: TechFlow customer support hours are stated in Eastern Time. Support runs Monday to Friday from 8 am to 8 pm Eastern Time, so customers in other time zones should convert the hours accordingly.

Q: Does TechFlow offer live chat support?
A: Yes, TechFlow offers live chat on its website for customers on the Plus and Premium support plans. Chat is staffed during regular support hours, Monday to Friday 8 am to 8 pm Eastern Time.

Q: Can I call TechFlow customer support by phone?
A: Phone support is included with the Premium plan ($79 per month). Premium customers can call during support hours, and also 24/7 for urgent P1 issues.

Q: Can Basic plan customers use phone or live chat support?
A: No, the free TechFlow Basic support plan includes email support only. Customers who want live chat can upgrade to Plus, and customers who want phone support can upgrade to Premium.

Q: Does TechFlow have a community forum?
A: Yes, TechFlow runs a community forum where customers can ask questions, share tips, and discuss features with other users. The forum is open to everyone and is linked from the TechFlow help center at help.techflow.example.

Q: Where is the TechFlow help center?
A: The TechFlow help center is at help.techflow.example. It contains setup guides, troubleshooting articles, policy pages, and answers to common customer support questions.

Q: Which languages does TechFlow customer support speak?
A: TechFlow customer support is provided in English. Customers may write in other languages, and TechFlow will do its best to respond, but response times are guaranteed for English requests.

Q: What are the TechFlow support plans?
A: TechFlow offers three support plans: Basic (free, email only), Plus ($19 per month, email and live chat), and Premium ($79 per month, adds phone and 24/7 P1 support). Enterprise customers also get a named account manager.

Q: What does the TechFlow Basic support plan include?
A: The TechFlow Basic support plan is free and includes email support only. The first response target is within 2 business days.

Q: What does the TechFlow Plus support plan include?
A: The TechFlow Plus support plan costs $19 per month and includes email and live chat support. The first response target is within 8 business hours.

Q: What does the TechFlow Premium support plan include?
A: The TechFlow Premium support plan costs $79 per month and includes email, live chat and phone support, plus 24/7 phone support for urgent P1 issues. First response is within 1 hour for P1 issues and within 4 business hours for all other priorities.

Q: How much does TechFlow Plus support cost?
A: TechFlow Plus support costs $19 per month. It adds live chat to the email support that comes with the free Basic plan.

Q: How much does TechFlow Premium support cost?
A: TechFlow Premium support costs $79 per month. It includes phone support, live chat, email, and 24/7 phone support for urgent P1 issues.

Q: Is the TechFlow Basic support plan really free?
A: Yes, the TechFlow Basic support plan is free for all customers and includes email support. There is no fee and no credit card is required to use it.

Q: How do I upgrade my TechFlow support plan?
A: To upgrade your TechFlow support plan, contact TechFlow customer support by email or live chat and name the plan you want, or use the plan settings in your account. The new plan applies from the next billing cycle or sooner if support confirms it.

Q: Can I downgrade or cancel my TechFlow support plan?
A: Yes, you can downgrade or cancel a paid TechFlow support plan at any time by contacting customer support. The plan stays active until the end of the period you already paid for.

Q: What is the first response time for Basic support?
A: TechFlow Basic support targets a first response within 2 business days. Basic is email only.

Q: What is the first response time for Plus support?
A: TechFlow Plus support targets a first response within 8 business hours. This applies to both email and live chat requests.

Q: What is the first response time for Premium support?
A: TechFlow Premium support targets a first response within 1 hour for P1 issues and within 4 business hours for all other priorities. P1 response is available 24/7 by phone.

Q: What counts as a business hour for TechFlow support response times?
A: A TechFlow business hour is an hour within support hours, Monday to Friday 8 am to 8 pm Eastern Time, excluding the 12 company holidays. Time outside these hours does not count toward response targets, except Premium P1 issues.

Q: Is a first response the same as a resolution?
A: No, the TechFlow first response time is the time until a support agent first replies to your ticket. It is a response target, not a guarantee that your issue will be resolved in that time.

Q: What are the TechFlow support priority levels?
A: TechFlow customer support uses four priority levels: P1 is service down or data loss, P2 is a major feature broken with no workaround, P3 is a minor issue or question, and P4 is a feature request.

Q: What does a P1 priority mean at TechFlow?
A: A TechFlow P1 ticket means the service is down or there is data loss. P1 is the most urgent level and is the only level eligible for 24/7 phone support on the Premium plan.

Q: What does a P2 priority mean at TechFlow?
A: A TechFlow P2 ticket means a major feature is broken and there is no workaround. It is handled after P1 issues and before minor issues.

Q: What does a P3 priority mean at TechFlow?
A: A TechFlow P3 ticket is a minor issue or a general question where you can keep working normally. Most customer questions are P3.

Q: What does a P4 priority mean at TechFlow?
A: A TechFlow P4 ticket is a feature request. It is logged for the product team rather than treated as a fault.

Q: What counts as urgent for TechFlow customer support?
A: TechFlow treats an issue as urgent when it is P1, meaning the service is down or data is lost. A broken feature with a workaround, or a minor issue, is not urgent.

Q: Who decides the priority of my TechFlow support ticket?
A: You suggest a priority when you open the ticket, and TechFlow customer support confirms or adjusts it based on the P1 to P4 definitions. Support will tell you if the priority is changed and why.

Q: Can I raise the priority of my ticket?
A: Yes, if your situation gets worse, for example a P2 problem now causes data loss, tell TechFlow customer support and ask for a re-evaluation. If it meets the P1 or P2 definition, the priority will be raised.

Q: How do I open a TechFlow support ticket?
A: To open a TechFlow support ticket, send an email to support@techflow.example or start a live chat on the website if your plan includes it. A ticket is created automatically and you receive a ticket number by email.

Q: How do I track the status of my support ticket?
A: You can track a TechFlow support ticket by replying to the confirmation email or by contacting customer support with your ticket number. Support will tell you the current status and next steps.

Q: How do I update an existing support ticket?
A: To update a TechFlow support ticket, reply to the ticket email thread or mention your ticket number in live chat. Replies are added to the same ticket so the history stays in one place.

Q: How do I close a support ticket?
A: To close a TechFlow support ticket, tell customer support that your issue is solved, or simply stop replying. Tickets are closed automatically after a period of no reply from the customer, and you can reopen one by answering.

Q: Can I reopen a closed support ticket?
A: Yes, reply to the closed TechFlow ticket email or quote your ticket number to customer support and the ticket will be reopened. If a lot of time has passed, support may open a new linked ticket instead.

Q: What information should I include in a support ticket?
A: Include your account email, order number if relevant, a clear description of the problem, the steps that led to it, any error messages, and screenshots or photos. TechFlow customer support can resolve tickets faster with complete details.

Q: What should I include when reporting a problem with a TechFlow Hub?
A: Include your order number, the device serial number, what you expected, what happened instead, and photos or a short video of the problem. This helps TechFlow customer support decide whether it is a warranty case.

Q: Does TechFlow need my password to help me?
A: No, TechFlow customer support will never ask for your password or full payment card number. If anyone asks for them, do not share them and report it to support@techflow.example.

Q: How do I escalate a support ticket?
A: To escalate a TechFlow ticket, reply to it asking for escalation and explain the business impact, or ask the support agent to involve a team lead. Premium and Enterprise customers can also contact their phone line or account manager.

Q: How do I make a complaint about TechFlow customer support?
A: Send your complaint to support@techflow.example with the subject line Complaint and your ticket number if you have one. A support supervisor reviews it and replies, usually within a few business days.

Q: What happens after I file a complaint?
A: After you file a complaint, a TechFlow customer support supervisor reviews your ticket history, contacts you for any missing details, and proposes a resolution. You are told the outcome by email.

Q: What is not covered by TechFlow customer support?
A: TechFlow customer support does not cover custom code you wrote, third-party integrations and plugins, or issues caused by modifications we did not make. Support can still point you to documentation that may help.

Q: Does TechFlow support third-party integrations?
A: TechFlow customer support helps with the TechFlow side of an integration, such as settings and API errors. We cannot troubleshoot the third-party product itself, which must be handled by its vendor.

Q: Does TechFlow support help with custom code?
A: TechFlow customer support does not debug or write custom code. We can confirm that the TechFlow platform behaves as documented and point you to API documentation in the help center.

Q: Does TechFlow customer support offer training for customers?
A: Yes, TechFlow offers onboarding guides, tutorials, and webinars through the help center for all customers. Enterprise customers can also arrange training sessions through their account manager.

Q: Is there an onboarding process for new TechFlow customers?
A: New TechFlow customers get a welcome email with setup guides and links to the help center. Enterprise customers also receive guided onboarding from their named account manager.

Q: What is a TechFlow account manager?
A: A TechFlow account manager is a named contact assigned to Enterprise customers. The account manager helps with onboarding, plan reviews, escalations, and coordinating with customer support.

Q: Who gets a named account manager?
A: Enterprise customers get a named TechFlow account manager. Basic, Plus, and Premium support plan customers use the standard support channels instead.

Q: How long is the TechFlow hardware warranty?
A: TechFlow hardware, including the TechFlow Hub and accessories sold in the TechFlow Store, has a 1-year warranty from the delivery date. It covers defects in materials and workmanship.

Q: When does the warranty period start?
A: The TechFlow hardware warranty starts on the delivery date of your order, not the order date. Keep your delivery confirmation and order number as proof.

Q: What does the TechFlow warranty cover?
A: The TechFlow warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship for 1 year from the delivery date. If a product is defective, TechFlow replaces or repairs it at no cost.

Q: What is not covered by the TechFlow warranty?
A: The TechFlow warranty does not cover accidental damage, water damage, or damage from unauthorized repair. Normal wear and tear is also not a defect in materials or workmanship.

Q: How do I make a warranty claim?
A: To make a TechFlow warranty claim, contact customer support with your order number, serial number, and a description or photos of the defect. If approved, support arranges a replacement or repair.

Q: Does the warranty claim cost anything?
A: No, approved TechFlow warranty claims are free, including shipping. TechFlow provides a free prepaid label for sending back a defective item.

Q: Will TechFlow replace or repair my defective product?
A: TechFlow will replace or repair a defective product at no cost, and decides which is appropriate. A replacement is usually sent when repair would take too long or is not possible.

Q: Does a replacement item get a new warranty?
A: A replacement TechFlow item is covered for the remainder of the original 1-year warranty period. If that period is short, support may extend it, and you can confirm this when you submit your claim.

Q: Does the TechFlow warranty cover damage I caused by dropping the device?
A: No, accidental damage such as drops is not covered by the TechFlow warranty. Customer support can still advise on possible paid repair options.

Q: Does water damage void the TechFlow warranty?
A: Water damage is not covered by the TechFlow warranty. If liquid damage is found on a returned device, the warranty claim will be declined.

Q: Does opening or repairing the device myself void the warranty?
A: Yes, unauthorized repair is excluded from the TechFlow warranty. Please contact TechFlow customer support rather than opening the device or using an unapproved repair shop.

Q: What is the TechFlow return policy?
A: TechFlow offers a 30-day return window from the delivery date. Items must be unused and in their original packaging.

Q: How long do I have to return a TechFlow Store product?
A: You have 30 days from the delivery date to return a TechFlow Store product. After that, only warranty claims for defects are accepted.

Q: What items are eligible for return?
A: TechFlow Store items are eligible for return if they are unused, in the original packaging, and returned within 30 days of delivery. Items that are used or missing packaging may be refused or partially refunded.

Q: How do I start a return?
A: To start a TechFlow return, contact customer support with your order number. Support issues a return authorization (RMA) number, which you must include with the package.

Q: What is an RMA number?
A: An RMA, or return authorization number, is a code TechFlow customer support issues to approve your return. It is valid for 14 days.

Q: How long is an RMA number valid?
A: A TechFlow RMA number is valid for 14 days from the day customer support issues it. If it expires, contact support to get a new one, as long as you are still within the 30-day return window.

Q: Can I return an item without an RMA number?
A: No, TechFlow returns need an RMA number from customer support. Packages sent without one may be delayed or refused.

Q: How much does return shipping cost?
A: Return shipping costs $7.95 for a standard TechFlow return. It is free if the item is defective or you received the wrong item.

Q: Do I pay for return shipping if the item is defective or wrong?
A: No, if the TechFlow item is defective or the wrong item was sent, TechFlow provides a free prepaid return label. Contact customer support to receive it.

Q: When will I get my refund?
A: TechFlow refunds are issued within 5 to 7 business days after the returned item is received. The refund is made to your original payment method.

Q: How will my refund be paid?
A: TechFlow refunds go to the original payment method used for the order. We cannot refund to a different card or account.

Q: Does TechFlow charge a restocking fee?
A: No, TechFlow does not charge a restocking fee for eligible returns. The only cost is the $7.95 return shipping, unless the item is defective or wrong.

Q: Can I get a partial refund?
A: TechFlow may issue a partial refund if a returned item shows signs of use or is missing original packaging or parts. Customer support will explain the amount before it is processed.

Q: Is original shipping refunded when I return an item?
A: Return shipping and original shipping are handled separately. For wrong or defective items, TechFlow covers all shipping costs, and for other returns, customer support will confirm what is refundable when issuing the RMA. See the TechFlow shipping policies FAQ for details.

Q: Can I exchange a TechFlow product for a different one?
A: Yes, contact TechFlow customer support within the 30-day return window and request an exchange. Support issues an RMA, and you can place a new order for the item you want.

Q: How do I cancel a TechFlow software subscription?
A: You can cancel a TechFlow software subscription anytime from your account settings or by contacting customer support. The subscription stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

Q: Do I get a refund when I cancel a monthly subscription?
A: No, monthly TechFlow software plans are not refunded for the current month. After cancelling, you can keep using the software until the end of the paid month.

Q: Can I get a refund on an annual subscription?
A: Yes, TechFlow annual plans are refundable within 14 days of purchase. After 14 days, you can cancel, but the plan stays active until the end of the year and is not refunded.

Q: Will my subscription stop immediately if I cancel?
A: No, a cancelled TechFlow subscription stays active until the end of the paid period. It will not renew after that.

Q: What if I was charged after cancelling my subscription?
A: Contact TechFlow customer support with your invoice number and cancellation date. If you were charged after a valid cancellation, support will correct it and refund the charge.

Q: I received the wrong item in my TechFlow Store order. What should I do?
A: Contact TechFlow customer support with your order number and a photo of the item received. Support will send a free prepaid return label and arrange the correct item.

Q: An item is missing from my order. What should I do?
A: Contact TechFlow customer support with your order number and a description of what is missing, ideally with a photo of the package contents. Support will check the order and send the missing item or refund it.

Q: What should I do if my TechFlow product arrives defective?
A: Contact TechFlow customer support with your order number and photos or a video of the defect. Defective items are replaced or repaired at no cost, with free prepaid shipping.

Q: Where is my order?
A: Check the tracking link in your TechFlow order confirmation email first. Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days and express 1 to 2 business days, and the TechFlow shipping policies FAQ has full delivery details.

Q: My package has not arrived. When should I contact support?
A: Contact TechFlow customer support if your package shows no tracking movement for 7 business days. Support will open a carrier investigation and arrange a replacement or refund if needed.

Q: My package arrived damaged. How do I report it?
A: Report damage to TechFlow customer support within 7 days of delivery, with photos of the packaging and the item. Support will arrange a replacement or refund.

Q: How do I get a copy of my invoice?
A: Invoices for TechFlow Store orders and software subscriptions are emailed after payment and can be found in your account. If you cannot find one, contact customer support with your order number and we will resend it.

Q: How do I dispute a charge on my invoice?
A: Contact TechFlow customer support with your invoice number and a description of the charge you dispute. Support will review it and correct or explain the charge.

Q: Can I change the billing details on an invoice?
A: Yes, contact TechFlow customer support to update the company name, address, or tax number on future invoices. Corrections to past invoices can be requested for a reasonable period after issue.

Q: Should I file a chargeback with my bank?
A: We ask that you contact TechFlow customer support first, because most billing problems are fixed quickly without a chargeback. If you do file one, support will provide the records needed for the review.

Q: Where can I get help with logging in to my TechFlow account?
A: For login problems, see the TechFlow Authentication and SSO FAQs, which cover passwords, multi-factor authentication, and single sign-on. If those do not help, contact customer support.

Q: Can TechFlow support reset my password for me?
A: For safety, you should use the self-service password reset described in the TechFlow Auth FAQ. If you cannot, customer support can help after verifying your identity.

Q: How does TechFlow customer support verify my identity?
A: TechFlow customer support verifies identity by checking that the request comes from your registered account email and may ask for details such as your order number. Support will never ask for your password.

Q: How do I request an export of my data?
A: Contact TechFlow customer support and ask for a data export from your account. Data requests are completed within 30 days.

Q: How do I ask TechFlow to delete my data?
A: Contact TechFlow customer support and request deletion of your personal data. Data deletion requests are completed within 30 days, and support confirms when it is done.

Q: How long do data requests take?
A: TechFlow completes data requests, both export and deletion, within 30 days of receiving and verifying the request.

Q: How do I report a security incident to TechFlow?
A: Report a suspected security incident immediately to support@techflow.example with the word Security in the subject, describing what you saw. If service is down or data is at risk, it is treated as P1.

Q: What should I do if I think my account was hacked?
A: Contact TechFlow customer support right away and change your password using the steps in the Auth FAQ. Support will treat it as a security incident and help secure your account.

Q: Does TechFlow customer support offer accessibility assistance?
A: Yes, TechFlow customer support can help customers with accessibility needs, for example by providing guides in alternative formats or helping with accessible setup. Contact support@techflow.example and describe what you need.

Q: How do I submit a feature request?
A: Send your idea to TechFlow customer support or post it in the community forum. Feature requests are logged as P4 and passed to the product team.

Q: Will my feature request be implemented?
A: TechFlow reviews every feature request, but we cannot promise that a requested feature will be built or give a delivery date. Requests are weighed against demand and product plans.

Q: How do I report a bug in the TechFlow software?
A: Contact TechFlow customer support with the steps to reproduce the bug, what you expected, what happened, and any error messages or screenshots. Priority depends on impact, from P1 to P3.

Q: How can I give feedback about TechFlow products?
A: You can give product feedback by emailing support@techflow.example, posting in the TechFlow community forum, or answering the satisfaction survey. All feedback is shared with the relevant teams.

Q: Where can I check if TechFlow services are down?
A: Check the TechFlow status page at status.techflow.example for current outages and maintenance. It shows incidents in real time and updates as they are resolved.

Q: What should I do during a TechFlow service outage?
A: Check status.techflow.example first, since known outages are listed there. If the outage is not listed and your service is down, contact customer support and report it as P1.

Q: Will I be notified about outages?
A: TechFlow posts outage and maintenance updates on the status page at status.techflow.example. You can also subscribe to updates there.

Q: Will I get a credit if there is an outage?
A: TechFlow reviews credit requests for major outages case by case. Contact customer support, and Enterprise customers can also ask their account manager.

Q: What is the TechFlow customer satisfaction survey?
A: The TechFlow customer satisfaction survey is a short questionnaire sent after a support ticket closes. It asks how well customer support resolved your issue.

Q: When do I receive the satisfaction survey?
A: TechFlow sends the satisfaction survey after your support ticket closes. You can ignore it, and responding is optional.

Q: Does TechFlow record support calls?
A: Yes, TechFlow may record support calls for quality and training purposes. You are told at the start of the call.

Q: Can I refuse to have my support call recorded?
A: Yes, tell the TechFlow agent at the start of the call that you do not want to be recorded, and they will explain the options. You can also use email or chat instead.

Q: How long does TechFlow keep support tickets?
A: TechFlow keeps support tickets for 24 months. After that, they are deleted unless required by law.

Q: Can I ask TechFlow to delete my support ticket history sooner?
A: Yes, you can ask TechFlow customer support to delete your ticket history as part of a data deletion request, completed within 30 days. Some billing records may be kept if the law requires.

Q: Does TechFlow offer goodwill gestures?
A: Yes, TechFlow customer support may offer a goodwill gesture, such as a discount, credit, or free shipping, when something went wrong. These are discretionary and not guaranteed.

Q: Can I get compensation for a bad support experience?
A: If TechFlow customer support fell short, send a complaint to support@techflow.example. A supervisor reviews it and may offer a goodwill gesture at their discretion.

Q: What is the TechFlow policy on abusive behavior?
A: TechFlow customer support treats customers with respect and asks for the same in return. Threats, harassment, or abusive language can lead to the conversation ending and, if repeated, restrictions on support channels.

Q: What happens if a customer is abusive to support staff?
A: TechFlow will warn the customer and may end the call or chat. Repeated abuse can lead to support being limited to email only.

Q: Can I speak with a human instead of a bot?
A: Yes, ask TechFlow customer support to connect you to a human agent. Live chat and phone are staffed by people during support hours.

Q: Does TechFlow support the TechFlow Hub setup?
A: Yes, TechFlow customer support helps with TechFlow Hub setup and troubleshooting, and the help center has setup guides. Support covers the Hub and TechFlow accessories, not third-party devices connected to them.

Q: Do I need an order number to contact support?
A: An order number is not required to contact TechFlow customer support, but it is needed for order, return, and warranty requests. For software questions, your account email is usually enough.

Q: Do support plans apply to the TechFlow Store hardware warranty?
A: No, the TechFlow hardware warranty is separate from support plans and applies to every Store purchase for 1 year from delivery. Basic, Plus, and Premium plans affect response times and channels only.
