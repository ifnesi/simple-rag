# Shipping Policies FAQ

Q: What shipping options does the TechFlow Store offer?
A: The TechFlow Store offers two shipping options: Standard shipping (3 to 5 business days) and Express shipping (1 to 2 business days). Overnight shipping is not offered.

Q: How much does standard shipping cost at the TechFlow Store?
A: TechFlow Store standard shipping costs $5.95 per order, and it is free on US orders of $50 or more.

Q: How much does express shipping cost at the TechFlow Store?
A: TechFlow Store express shipping costs a flat $14.95 per order and delivers in 1 to 2 business days after the order ships.

Q: Does the TechFlow Store offer overnight shipping?
A: No, the TechFlow Store does not offer overnight shipping. The fastest option is express shipping, which takes 1 to 2 business days.

Q: What is the free shipping threshold at the TechFlow Store?
A: TechFlow Store standard shipping is free on US orders of $50 or more. US orders under $50 pay $5.95 for standard shipping.

Q: Does free shipping apply to express orders?
A: No, the free shipping offer at the TechFlow Store applies only to standard shipping on US orders of $50 or more. Express shipping always costs $14.95.

Q: Does free shipping apply to Canada and UK orders?
A: No, free standard shipping at the TechFlow Store is available only on US orders of $50 or more. Orders to Canada and the United Kingdom are charged shipping at checkout.

Q: Is the $50 free shipping threshold calculated before or after discounts?
A: The TechFlow Store free shipping threshold is based on the order subtotal shown at checkout. If the total of your items is $50 or more for a US order, standard shipping is free; the checkout page shows the exact shipping charge before you pay.

Q: How long does it take TechFlow Store to process an order?
A: TechFlow Store orders have a processing time of 1 business day. Processing includes picking, packing and handing the parcel to the carrier.

Q: What is the TechFlow Store order cut-off time for same-day shipping?
A: TechFlow Store orders placed before 2:00 pm Eastern Time on a business day ship the same day. Orders placed after 2:00 pm Eastern Time ship the next business day.

Q: What happens if I order on a weekend?
A: TechFlow Store orders placed on a Saturday or Sunday are processed on the next business day. The 1 business day processing time starts then, and your tracking number is emailed when the order ships.

Q: Does the TechFlow Store ship on weekends?
A: No, TechFlow Store orders ship on business days only, Monday to Friday. Orders placed on weekends or holidays ship on the next business day.

Q: What counts as a business day for TechFlow Store shipping?
A: A business day for TechFlow Store shipping is Monday through Friday, excluding public holidays. Weekends and holidays are not counted in processing or delivery estimates.

Q: Does the delivery estimate include the processing time?
A: No, TechFlow Store delivery estimates start after the order ships. Add the 1 business day of processing time to the shipping method estimate for the full time to arrive.

Q: How long does standard shipping take within the United States?
A: TechFlow Store standard shipping within the United States takes 3 to 5 business days after the order ships.

Q: How long does express shipping take within the United States?
A: TechFlow Store express shipping within the United States takes 1 to 2 business days after the order ships.

Q: Which countries does the TechFlow Store ship to?
A: The TechFlow Store ships to the United States, Canada and the United Kingdom. We do not currently ship to any other countries.

Q: How long does shipping to Canada take?
A: TechFlow Store standard shipping to Canada takes 6 to 10 business days after the order ships. Canada Post delivers the final leg.

Q: How long does shipping to the United Kingdom take?
A: TechFlow Store standard shipping to the United Kingdom takes 7 to 12 business days after the order ships. Royal Mail delivers the final leg.

Q: Is express shipping available for international orders?
A: No, express shipping is not offered for international TechFlow Store orders. Orders to Canada and the United Kingdom ship with standard international delivery only.

Q: Which carriers does the TechFlow Store use?
A: The TechFlow Store ships with UPS and USPS in the United States, Canada Post in Canada, and Royal Mail in the United Kingdom.

Q: Can I choose between UPS and USPS for my US order?
A: No, TechFlow Store chooses UPS or USPS for each US order based on the package and destination. You cannot select a specific carrier at checkout, only standard or express shipping.

Q: Will I receive a tracking number for my TechFlow Store order?
A: Yes, a tracking number is emailed to you when your TechFlow Store order ships. The email contains a link to follow the parcel with the carrier.

Q: When will I get my tracking number?
A: TechFlow Store emails your tracking number as soon as the order ships, which is the same day for orders placed before 2:00 pm Eastern Time on a business day. Otherwise it arrives the next business day.

Q: I have not received a tracking email. What should I do?
A: First check your spam or junk folder for the TechFlow Store shipping email. If your order is more than 2 business days old and there is still no tracking email, contact customer support at support@techflow.example.

Q: My tracking number says no information found. Is something wrong?
A: Carriers can take up to 24 hours after a TechFlow Store order ships to show the first scan. If tracking still shows nothing after 2 business days, contact customer support.

Q: My tracking has not updated for several days. What should I do?
A: Short pauses in tracking are normal for TechFlow Store parcels in transit. If tracking shows no movement for 7 business days, contact customer support and we will reship or refund the order.

Q: Where can I track my TechFlow Store package?
A: Use the link in your TechFlow Store shipping email or paste the tracking number on the website of the carrier shown: UPS or USPS in the US, Canada Post in Canada, or Royal Mail in the UK.

Q: Does the TechFlow Store send shipment notifications?
A: Yes, the TechFlow Store sends an email when your order ships, with your tracking number and carrier. You can also follow the parcel through the carrier's own tracking page.

Q: Can I get text message updates for my delivery?
A: The TechFlow Store sends shipment notifications by email. For text message alerts, you can enter your tracking number with the carrier, which may offer its own delivery notifications.

Q: Can I cancel my TechFlow Store order?
A: Yes, but only until the order ships, which is usually within 2 hours of ordering. Contact customer support right away at support@techflow.example, by live chat or by phone.

Q: How long do I have to cancel a TechFlow Store order?
A: TechFlow Store orders can be cancelled only until they ship, usually within 2 hours of placing the order. After the order ships it cannot be cancelled, but you can return it under the 30-day return policy.

Q: What if my order has already shipped and I want to cancel it?
A: A shipped TechFlow Store order cannot be cancelled. When it arrives, you can return it within 30 days of delivery if the items are unused and in original packaging.

Q: Can I change the items in my order after placing it?
A: Item changes to a TechFlow Store order are possible only before it ships, usually within 2 hours of ordering. Contact customer support quickly, or cancel and place a new order.

Q: Can I change the delivery address after ordering?
A: Yes, but only until the TechFlow Store order ships, which is usually within 2 hours of ordering. Contact customer support right away with your order number and the new address.

Q: Can I change the delivery address after my order has shipped?
A: No, TechFlow Store cannot change the delivery address once an order has shipped. You may be able to arrange a redirect directly with the carrier using your tracking number.

Q: Can I change my shipping method after placing an order?
A: A shipping method change on a TechFlow Store order is possible only before it ships, usually within 2 hours of ordering. Contact customer support, who can update it if the order has not yet shipped.

Q: Does the TechFlow Store ship to PO boxes?
A: Yes, standard shipping to US PO boxes is possible with USPS. Express shipping and UPS deliveries require a street address.

Q: Does the TechFlow Store ship to APO and FPO addresses?
A: Yes, TechFlow Store ships to APO and FPO military addresses via USPS with standard shipping. Delivery to these addresses can take longer than the usual 3 to 5 business days.

Q: Can I ship to a business address?
A: Yes, the TechFlow Store delivers to business addresses. Include a company name and suite number, and make sure someone is available to receive the parcel during business hours.

Q: Can I ship to an apartment or shared building?
A: Yes, the TechFlow Store delivers to apartments. Enter your unit number in the address line and, if needed, add door or buzzer instructions so the carrier can reach you.

Q: Can I ship to a hotel or a relative's home?
A: Yes, the TechFlow Store can ship to any valid street address in the United States, Canada or the United Kingdom. For hotels, add the guest name and check-in dates in the address notes.

Q: Can I ship to a parcel locker or carrier pickup point?
A: The TechFlow Store does not guarantee delivery to carrier parcel lockers or pickup points. We recommend a street address or a US PO box for standard orders.

Q: Do TechFlow Store packages require a signature?
A: Most TechFlow Store packages are delivered without a signature. Higher-value orders may require an adult signature at delivery, which is shown in your tracking details.

Q: Can I request a signature on my delivery?
A: Contact customer support before your TechFlow Store order ships if you would like a signature required. Once the parcel is with the carrier, you can ask the carrier about delivery instructions using your tracking number.

Q: What if nobody is home when the carrier delivers?
A: The carrier will leave the TechFlow Store parcel in a safe place or attempt delivery again, depending on the service. A notice with pickup or redelivery instructions is usually left.

Q: How many delivery attempts does the carrier make?
A: Carriers usually make up to 3 delivery attempts for a TechFlow Store parcel before holding it or returning it. The exact number depends on the carrier and country, so check your tracking page.

Q: I missed my delivery. What can I do?
A: Follow the redelivery or pickup instructions on the carrier's notice, or use your TechFlow Store tracking number on the carrier's website to schedule another attempt. Contact customer support if the parcel is returned to us.

Q: What happens if a package is held at a carrier location?
A: Carriers hold TechFlow Store parcels at a local facility for a limited time, usually about 5 to 15 days. Collect it with photo ID and your tracking number, or the parcel may be sent back to us.

Q: What happens if my package is returned to TechFlow Store as undeliverable?
A: When a parcel is returned to the TechFlow Store as undeliverable, contact customer support. We can reship to a corrected address, with any new shipping charge, or refund the items.

Q: What if I entered the wrong address?
A: If the TechFlow Store order has not shipped, contact customer support immediately to fix the address. If it has shipped, contact the carrier with your tracking number, as a wrong address may lead to a lost or returned parcel.

Q: My tracking says delivered but I did not receive the package. What should I do?
A: First check around your property, with neighbors and with your building's front desk. If the TechFlow Store parcel is still missing after 1 business day, contact customer support.

Q: What counts as a lost package?
A: A TechFlow Store package is considered lost when tracking shows no movement for 7 business days. Contact customer support at that point and we will reship or refund your order.

Q: What will TechFlow Store do if my package is lost?
A: If tracking shows no movement for 7 business days, contact customer support. TechFlow Store will reship the items or issue a refund, whichever you prefer where stock allows.

Q: What should I do if my package arrives damaged?
A: Report damage to the TechFlow Store within 7 days of delivery by contacting customer support with photos of the box and the product. We will then arrange a replacement or refund.

Q: How long do I have to report a damaged shipment?
A: TechFlow Store damaged packages must be reported within 7 days of delivery, and the report must include clear photos of the packaging and damage. Reports after that period may not be eligible.

Q: Do I pay to return or replace an item damaged in transit?
A: No, you pay nothing. When a TechFlow Store item arrives damaged and is reported with photos within 7 days of delivery, we provide a free prepaid return label and ship the replacement at no cost.

Q: What happens if only part of my order arrives?
A: TechFlow Store may ship large orders in more than one package, each with its own tracking number. If a package is missing and tracking shows no movement for 7 business days, contact customer support.

Q: Does the TechFlow Store split orders into partial shipments?
A: Yes, when some items are in stock and others are not, TechFlow Store may ship the available items first. You receive a separate tracking email for each shipment.

Q: Do I pay extra shipping for a partial shipment?
A: No, TechFlow Store charges shipping only once per order, even when the order is split into partial shipments. The shipping rate you saw at checkout is the amount you pay.

Q: What happens if an item is backordered?
A: If an item in your TechFlow Store order is backordered, we tell you by email with the expected restock date. You can wait for it, receive the in-stock items first, or cancel the backordered item for a refund.

Q: Can I cancel a backordered item?
A: Yes, a backordered item in a TechFlow Store order can be cancelled for a refund to your original payment method, as it has not yet shipped. Contact customer support with your order number.

Q: Does the TechFlow Store offer pre-orders?
A: Yes, the TechFlow Store may offer pre-orders on new products. Pre-order items ship on the release date shown on the product page, and your tracking number is emailed when they ship.

Q: When will my pre-order ship?
A: TechFlow Store pre-orders ship on or shortly after the release date on the product page. Standard or express delivery times then apply from the ship date.

Q: If I order a pre-order item with an in-stock item, when does my order ship?
A: By default, the TechFlow Store ships in-stock items first and the pre-order item later, in a separate shipment. Shipping is charged once, and each package has its own tracking number.

Q: Can I cancel a pre-order?
A: Yes, a TechFlow Store pre-order can be cancelled any time before it ships. Contact customer support with your order number and the refund goes to your original payment method.

Q: Will I be charged duties or taxes on international orders?
A: Yes, duties and taxes on international TechFlow Store orders to Canada and the UK are paid by the customer at delivery. These charges are set by customs authorities and not collected by TechFlow Store.

Q: Who pays customs fees on a TechFlow Store order?
A: The customer pays customs duties, import taxes and any carrier handling fees at delivery for international TechFlow Store orders. TechFlow Store does not collect these at checkout.

Q: How much will the duties and taxes cost?
A: The amount is set by Canadian or UK customs and depends on your order value and local rules, so TechFlow Store cannot predict it. Check with your local customs authority or carrier for an estimate.

Q: What if I refuse to pay duties and taxes at delivery?
A: If you decline to pay duties and taxes, the carrier will return the international TechFlow Store parcel to us. Return shipping costs and original shipping charges may be deducted from any refund.

Q: How long does customs clearance take for international orders?
A: Customs clearance for TechFlow Store parcels to Canada and the UK normally takes a few days and is already counted in the 6 to 10 or 7 to 12 business day estimates. Occasional customs inspections can cause extra delay.

Q: Are there items the TechFlow Store cannot ship internationally?
A: Some TechFlow Store accessories that contain batteries may face carrier or customs restrictions in certain countries. If an item in your order cannot be shipped, customer support will contact you.

Q: Does the TechFlow Store ship to countries other than the US, Canada and the UK?
A: No, the TechFlow Store currently ships only to the United States, Canada and the United Kingdom. Orders to other countries cannot be placed at checkout.

Q: Can I ship to a freight forwarder or reshipping service?
A: The TechFlow Store does not support shipments to countries outside the US, Canada and the UK, even through a forwarder. If the forwarder address is inside those countries, the order ships as normal but delivery onward is your responsibility.

Q: Can I use my US address for a billing address on a Canadian delivery?
A: Yes, the billing and shipping addresses of a TechFlow Store order can differ. International duties and taxes are still paid at delivery based on the shipping destination.

Q: Does the TechFlow Store ship products with batteries?
A: Yes, the TechFlow Store ships devices and accessories that contain batteries in compliance with carrier safety rules. Batteries are packed securely and shipped only by approved services.

Q: Are there shipping restrictions on the TechFlow Hub?
A: The TechFlow Hub ships to the United States, Canada and the United Kingdom like other TechFlow Store products. It is packed in protective packaging, and battery rules, if any, are handled by TechFlow Store and the carrier.

Q: How are TechFlow Store products packaged?
A: TechFlow Store products are packed in sturdy boxes with protective inserts so the TechFlow Hub and accessories such as cables, stands and cases arrive safely.

Q: Is TechFlow Store packaging recyclable?
A: Yes, TechFlow Store shipping boxes and paper fillers are made to be recyclable. We avoid unnecessary plastic where we can while still protecting the product.

Q: Does the TechFlow Store use eco-friendly packaging?
A: TechFlow Store aims to use recycled cardboard and paper-based protective materials for its shipments. Please recycle the box and fillers locally after unpacking.

Q: Can I request no extra packaging or a gift-free invoice?
A: The TechFlow Store packs every order in a protective box, and we cannot remove packaging. You can request that no invoice is included in the parcel by contacting customer support before the order ships.

Q: Are there delays during the holiday season?
A: Yes, TechFlow Store orders may take longer during peak seasons like November and December because carriers handle many more parcels. Estimates can extend by a few business days.

Q: Do carriers delay deliveries around public holidays?
A: Yes, UPS, USPS, Canada Post and Royal Mail do not operate normally on public holidays. TechFlow Store delivery estimates count business days only, so holidays can add a day or more.

Q: What is the best time to order for holiday delivery?
A: For holiday gifts, order from the TechFlow Store as early as possible and choose express shipping, which takes 1 to 2 business days. Place orders before 2:00 pm Eastern Time for same-day shipping.

Q: Can bad weather delay my TechFlow Store delivery?
A: Yes, storms, snow, floods and other severe weather can delay carriers and your TechFlow Store parcel. Delays are outside our control, so follow your tracking page for updates.

Q: Will I get a refund for express shipping if my order is delayed by weather?
A: Express shipping refunds are not available for delays caused by weather or other events outside our control. If your TechFlow Store order is lost, contact customer support after 7 business days with no tracking movement.

Q: Does the TechFlow Store offer shipping insurance?
A: TechFlow Store parcels are covered against loss and damage in transit. You do not need to buy extra insurance, but you must report damage within 7 days of delivery, or loss after 7 business days with no tracking movement.

Q: Who is responsible for a package once it leaves the TechFlow Store?
A: TechFlow Store remains responsible for the parcel until it is delivered. If it is lost or damaged in transit, contact customer support and we will reship or refund.

Q: How do I start a return for a TechFlow Store order?
A: Contact customer support at support@techflow.example, by live chat or by phone, to start a return. Support issues a return authorization (RMA) number that is valid for 14 days.

Q: What is an RMA number?
A: An RMA, or return authorization number, is a code issued by TechFlow Store customer support when you start a return. It is valid for 14 days and must be included with your returned package.

Q: How long is an RMA number valid?
A: A TechFlow Store RMA number is valid for 14 days from the day it is issued. If it expires before you ship the return, contact customer support for a new one.

Q: How long do I have to return an item?
A: TechFlow Store accepts returns within 30 days of the delivery date. Items must be unused and in their original packaging.

Q: How much does return shipping cost?
A: TechFlow Store return shipping costs $7.95, which is deducted from your refund, unless the item is defective or the wrong item was sent. In those cases we provide a free prepaid return label.

Q: Who pays for return shipping if the item is defective?
A: TechFlow Store pays. When an item is defective, customer support provides a free prepaid return label and you pay no return shipping.

Q: Who pays for return shipping if I received the wrong item?
A: TechFlow Store pays for return shipping when we sent the wrong item. Customer support provides a free prepaid return label for the item.

Q: How do I get a return shipping label?
A: Contact TechFlow Store customer support to start a return. We issue an RMA number and the return label, which is free for defective or wrong items and $7.95 for other returns.

Q: How do I send a return back to TechFlow Store?
A: Pack the item unused in its original packaging, include your RMA number, attach the return label from customer support, and drop the parcel at the carrier named on the label.

Q: Can I use my own carrier to send a return?
A: We recommend the label from TechFlow Store customer support because it is tracked to our returns warehouse. If you use your own carrier, you pay the postage and are responsible for the parcel until we receive it.

Q: Should I keep proof of postage for my return?
A: Yes, keep the drop-off receipt and tracking number for your TechFlow Store return until your refund arrives. It proves you shipped the parcel if there is a delay.

Q: How long does it take for TechFlow Store to receive my return?
A: Returns usually reach TechFlow Store within the same time as standard shipping, about 3 to 5 business days in the US. Returns from Canada and the UK take longer, similar to international delivery times.

Q: When will I get my refund after returning an item?
A: TechFlow Store issues refunds to your original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after the return is received. Your bank may need extra time to show it.

Q: Do I get a refund for the original shipping charge?
A: The original shipping charge on a TechFlow Store order is refunded only if the item was defective or the wrong item was sent. Otherwise, only the item price is refunded, minus the $7.95 return shipping fee.

Q: Can I return a package from Canada or the UK?
A: Yes, TechFlow Store accepts returns from Canada and the UK within 30 days of delivery. Contact customer support to get an RMA number and return label, as the return shipping fee is $7.95 for non-defective items.

Q: Will duties and taxes be refunded on an international return?

Q: How do exchanges work for TechFlow Store orders?
A: To exchange an item, contact customer support, who issue an RMA number and arrange the return and replacement. Return shipping costs $7.95 unless the item is defective or wrong.

Q: Do I pay shipping for the exchange item?
A: For TechFlow Store exchanges of defective or wrong items, the replacement ships free. For other exchanges, the $7.95 return shipping fee applies and the new item is sent at the standard shipping rate.

Q: How fast will my replacement ship?
A: TechFlow Store ships replacements for defective items within 1 business day of receiving your return, or sooner when customer support approves an advance replacement. Delivery then takes 3 to 5 business days in the US.

Q: What if my TechFlow Hub arrives defective?

A: Business orders to a US address of $50 or more qualify for free standard shipping like any TechFlow Store order. For very large shipments, contact customer support to discuss delivery.

A: Have your TechFlow Store order number, the email used for the order, your tracking number and, for damage, photos of the package and product. This helps support resolve your shipping issue faster.
