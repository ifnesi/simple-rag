# TechFlow Solutions Employee Handbook

## What is our unlimited PTO policy?
TechFlow offers unlimited PTO with a minimum requirement of 15 days per year. Employees must get approval for time off exceeding 2 consecutive weeks. PTO requests require 2-week notice except for emergencies.

## How does our equity vesting schedule work?
New hires receive equity grants vesting over 5 years: 10% after year 1, then 22.5% each subsequent year. Equity grants are reviewed annually in March with potential for additional grants based on performance.

## Does unused PTO carry over to the next year at TechFlow?
Because TechFlow PTO is unlimited, there is no balance to carry over and unused days are not paid out. The 15-day annual minimum still applies to every employee each calendar year, so unused minimum days do not roll into the next year.

## How do employees request time off at TechFlow?
TechFlow employees submit a PTO request in the HR portal under the Time Off tab, choosing the dates and adding a short note. Requests should be filed at least 2 weeks before the first day off unless it is an emergency.

## Who approves PTO requests at TechFlow?
A TechFlow employee's direct manager approves PTO requests, usually within 2 business days. Time off longer than 2 consecutive weeks also needs a second approval from the department head.

## How many sick days do TechFlow employees get?
TechFlow gives every employee 10 paid sick days per year, and these do not count toward the unlimited PTO policy or the 15-day PTO minimum. Employees should tell their manager as early as possible on the day they are out.

## Do I need a doctor's note for sick leave at TechFlow?
TechFlow only asks for a doctor's note when an employee is out sick for more than 3 consecutive working days. For shorter absences, a message to the manager is enough.

## What is TechFlow's bereavement leave policy?
TechFlow employees receive up to 5 paid days of bereavement leave for the death of an immediate family member and 3 days for extended family. Bereavement days are separate from PTO and sick days, and no 2-week notice is required.

## Does TechFlow provide paid time off for jury duty?
Yes, TechFlow pays employees their regular salary for the full duration of jury duty or a court witness summons. Employees should share the summons with HR as soon as they receive it.

## What are floating holidays at TechFlow?
TechFlow employees get 2 floating holidays per year that they can use for cultural, religious or personal observances. Floating holidays are in addition to the 12 company holidays and do not reduce PTO.

## How many company holidays does TechFlow observe?
TechFlow observes 12 paid company holidays each year, and the office is closed on those days. The full list is published in the HR portal every December for the following year.

## Which days are TechFlow's company holidays?
TechFlow's 12 company holidays include New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving and the day after, and a winter break of three days around December 25. HR confirms the exact dates each year in the portal.

## What happens if a company holiday falls on a weekend at TechFlow?
When a TechFlow company holiday falls on a Saturday it is observed on the preceding Friday, and when it falls on a Sunday it is observed on the following Monday. Employees are not required to work on the observed day.

## Does PTO accrue while an employee is on leave at TechFlow?
TechFlow has no PTO accrual because PTO is unlimited, so being on leave does not reduce an employee's access to time off. The 15-day annual minimum is prorated for the part of the year an employee is actively working.

## How much paid parental leave does TechFlow offer?
TechFlow offers 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave to all employees, regardless of gender or whether the child arrives by birth, adoption or foster placement. The leave can be taken in one block or split into two blocks within the first 12 months.

## Does TechFlow parental leave apply to adoptive and foster parents?
Yes, TechFlow gives adoptive and foster parents the same 16 weeks of paid parental leave as birth parents. Employees should notify HR at least 30 days before the expected start when possible.

## Do employees get extra leave for childbirth recovery at TechFlow?
TechFlow provides an additional 6 weeks of paid medical leave for birth parents to recover from childbirth, on top of the 16 weeks of parental leave. This medical leave does not use sick days or PTO.

## What support does TechFlow offer when returning from parental leave?
TechFlow employees returning from parental leave can work a reduced schedule of 80% at full pay for their first 4 weeks back. The company also provides a private lactation room at the office and flexible hours for pumping when working remotely.

## Does TechFlow offer leave to care for a sick family member?
TechFlow gives employees up to 8 weeks of paid family caregiver leave per year to care for a spouse, partner, child, parent or sibling with a serious health condition. Employees should apply through HR with as much notice as possible.

## When do TechFlow equity grants start vesting?
TechFlow equity has a 1-year cliff, so nothing vests before an employee's first anniversary. On that date 10% of the grant vests at once, followed by 22.5% on each of the next four anniversaries.

## What does the 1-year cliff mean for TechFlow equity?
The cliff means a TechFlow employee who leaves before completing 12 months forfeits the entire equity grant. After the first anniversary, the 10% tranche vests and the rest follows the yearly schedule.

## What are refresh grants at TechFlow?
Refresh grants are additional TechFlow equity awards given to employees who perform strongly after their initial grant. They are considered during the annual March equity review and usually vest over 4 years.

## What happens to my TechFlow equity if I leave the company?
When a TechFlow employee leaves, unvested equity is forfeited on the last day of employment. Vested shares or options remain the employee's, subject to the exercise window.

## How long is the post-termination exercise window at TechFlow?
Former TechFlow employees have 90 days after their last day to exercise vested stock options. Employees with at least 4 years of service get an extended window of 12 months.

## What is the difference between options and RSUs at TechFlow?
Before TechFlow's IPO, equity is granted as stock options, which let employees buy shares at a fixed strike price. After a listing, new grants will be restricted stock units that convert to shares as they vest.

## How is TechFlow equity taxed?
Taxes depend on the type of award and the country, but in general exercising options or receiving vested RSUs can create taxable income. TechFlow recommends that employees consult a personal tax advisor and provides a 1-hour free tax consultation each year.

## Where can I view my TechFlow equity grants?
TechFlow employees can see their grant size, vesting dates and vested balance in the equity portal, linked from the HR portal home page. The portal is updated within 5 business days of each vesting date.

## Does TechFlow accelerate equity vesting in an acquisition?
If TechFlow is acquired, employees receive 25% acceleration of their remaining unvested equity. Employees who are terminated without cause within 12 months of the acquisition receive full acceleration.

## Can TechFlow employees sell their shares?
TechFlow is a private company, so employees can sell shares only during company-organized tender offers, which are announced about once a year. Employees cannot sell shares privately without written approval from the legal team.

## What health insurance does TechFlow offer?
TechFlow offers a choice of three medical plans (PPO, HMO and a high-deductible plan with an HSA). The company pays 90% of the premium for employees and 75% for dependents.

## When does health insurance start for new TechFlow employees?
Health, dental and vision coverage for new TechFlow employees begins on the first day of the month after they start. Employees have 30 days from their start date to enroll.

## What dental coverage does TechFlow provide?
TechFlow's dental plan covers 100% of preventive care, 80% of basic procedures and 50% of major procedures, with an annual maximum of $2,000 per person. The company pays 90% of the employee premium.

## What vision coverage does TechFlow provide?
TechFlow's vision plan covers one eye exam per year and an allowance of $200 toward glasses or contact lenses. Employees can add dependents at a low monthly premium.

## Can I add my spouse or children to TechFlow health insurance?
Yes, TechFlow employees can add a spouse, domestic partner and children up to age 26 during enrollment or within 30 days of a qualifying life event. Dependents are covered at a 75% company premium contribution.

## When is open enrollment at TechFlow?
TechFlow's open enrollment runs for two weeks every November, and changes take effect on January 1. Outside that period, employees can change plans only after a qualifying life event such as marriage or a birth.

## What is an HSA and does TechFlow contribute to it?
An HSA is a tax-advantaged health savings account available to employees on TechFlow's high-deductible plan. TechFlow contributes $600 per year for individuals and $1,200 for families.

## Does TechFlow offer a 401(k) plan?
Yes, TechFlow offers a 401(k) plan that employees can join on their first day of employment. Employees choose their contribution percentage in the benefits portal and can change it at any time.

## How much does TechFlow match on 401(k) contributions?
TechFlow matches 100% of employee 401(k) contributions up to 4% of eligible pay. For example, an employee who contributes 4% receives an additional 4% from the company.

## When does the TechFlow 401(k) match vest?
The TechFlow 401(k) match is 100% vested immediately, so employees keep all matched funds even if they leave. Employees' own contributions are always fully theirs.

## Does TechFlow offer a Roth 401(k) option?
Yes, TechFlow employees can split their contributions between traditional pre-tax and Roth after-tax 401(k) accounts. The company match always goes into the traditional pre-tax account.

## What is the TechFlow wellness stipend?
TechFlow gives every employee a wellness stipend of $50 per month for gym memberships, fitness classes, meditation apps or similar. Employees submit receipts through the expense tool to be reimbursed.

## How much is the TechFlow learning budget?
TechFlow provides every employee a learning budget of $1,500 per year for courses, books, certifications and conferences. Unused budget does not roll over to the next year.

## How do I use my TechFlow learning budget?
TechFlow employees pay for the learning item and submit the receipt in the expense tool with the category Learning. Purchases above $500 need manager approval before buying.

## Does TechFlow offer a commuter benefit?
TechFlow reimburses up to $100 per month in public transit or parking costs for employees who commute to the office. Employees can also set aside additional pre-tax money through the commuter program.

## What is the TechFlow home-office stipend?
TechFlow gives each employee a one-time home-office stipend of $750 to buy a desk, chair, monitor or other furniture. The stipend is available after the first 30 days of employment.

## What is TechFlow's employee assistance program?
TechFlow's employee assistance program (EAP) offers free, confidential counseling, legal advice and financial coaching for employees and their household members. Employees get 8 free therapy sessions per issue per year.

## Does TechFlow offer mental health support?
Yes, besides the employee assistance program, TechFlow subsidizes a meditation app subscription and offers two company-wide mental health days per year. These mental health days are paid and separate from PTO and sick days.

## Does TechFlow offer life and disability insurance?
TechFlow provides basic life insurance equal to 2 times annual salary and short- and long-term disability coverage at no cost to employees. Employees can buy additional coverage at group rates.

## How often does TechFlow pay employees?
TechFlow pays employees twice a month, on the 15th and on the last business day of the month. If a payday falls on a weekend or holiday, payment is made on the preceding business day.

## How can I view my TechFlow pay stubs?
TechFlow employees can view and download pay stubs in the payroll portal, which is linked from the HR portal. Pay stubs are available the day before each payday.

## How do I change my direct deposit at TechFlow?
TechFlow employees can update bank details in the payroll portal under Direct Deposit. Changes made at least 5 business days before payday take effect on that payday.

## What if my TechFlow paycheck is wrong?
Employees who spot an error in their TechFlow paycheck should email payroll@techflow.example within 5 business days. Payroll corrects underpayments within 2 business days with an off-cycle payment.

## How can I update my tax withholding at TechFlow?
TechFlow employees can update their tax withholding forms in the payroll portal at any time. Changes apply from the next payroll cycle.

## How often does TechFlow review compensation?
TechFlow reviews employee compensation once a year in March, together with the equity review. Off-cycle adjustments are possible for promotions or significant role changes.

## How does TechFlow decide salary levels?
TechFlow sets salary using job levels and bands based on market data from salary surveys, updated every year. An employee's position within the band depends on skills, experience and performance.

## How often are performance reviews at TechFlow?
TechFlow runs performance reviews twice a year, in June and December. Each review includes a self-assessment, peer feedback and a manager evaluation.

## What are the performance ratings at TechFlow?
TechFlow uses four ratings: Exceeds Expectations, Meets Expectations, Partially Meets and Does Not Meet. Ratings inform compensation, bonuses and promotion decisions.

## How does promotion work at TechFlow?
TechFlow employees are promoted when they consistently perform at the next level, as shown by evidence in a promotion packet. Managers nominate employees, and a calibration committee decides twice a year, in March and September.

## Can I ask for a promotion at TechFlow?
Yes, TechFlow employees can discuss promotion readiness with their manager at any time and ask for the written level expectations. Employees can also request to be considered in the next promotion cycle.

## What happens if I get a poor performance review at TechFlow?
A TechFlow employee with a Partially Meets or Does Not Meet rating receives a 60-day performance improvement plan with clear goals and weekly check-ins. The employee also gets support such as coaching or training.

## Does TechFlow pay annual bonuses?
Yes, TechFlow offers an annual performance bonus with a target of 10% of base salary for most roles. Bonuses are paid in the March payroll based on company and individual results.

## Does TechFlow have a referral bonus?
TechFlow pays a $3,000 referral bonus when a referred candidate is hired and stays for 90 days. Engineering and other hard-to-fill roles pay $5,000.

## How do I refer a candidate at TechFlow?
TechFlow employees submit a referral in the careers tool with the candidate's name and resume before the candidate applies. Referrals submitted after an application cannot receive a bonus.

## Does TechFlow offer sign-on or spot bonuses?
TechFlow can grant spot bonuses of up to $1,000 for outstanding contributions, nominated by managers. Sign-on bonuses are offered only in certain hiring cases and must be repaid if the employee leaves within 12 months.

## What is TechFlow's remote work policy?
TechFlow is remote-friendly, and employees may work from home in their country of employment. Employees who live near an office are encouraged to visit at least once a month.

## What is the hybrid work arrangement at TechFlow?
Employees assigned to a hybrid role at TechFlow work from the office 2 days per week, Tuesday and Thursday. The remaining days can be worked from home.

## Can I work from another country at TechFlow?
TechFlow employees can work remotely from another country for up to 30 days per calendar year with manager approval. Longer stays need approval from HR and legal because of tax and visa implications.

## What are TechFlow's office hours?
TechFlow offices are open from 8:00 AM to 7:00 PM on weekdays, and employees can use badge access during those hours. Weekend access requires approval from the facilities team.

## What are TechFlow's core hours?
TechFlow's core hours are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in each employee's local time zone, when employees should be available for meetings. Outside core hours employees can set their own schedule.

## What laptop does TechFlow provide?
TechFlow provides every employee a company laptop, either a MacBook Pro or a Linux or Windows laptop based on role. IT ships the laptop to arrive before the first day of work.

## How often does TechFlow replace laptops?
TechFlow replaces employee laptops every 3 years, or sooner if the device is broken. Employees can request a replacement from the IT help desk.

## Can I use my personal device for work at TechFlow?
TechFlow employees may access email and chat from personal phones if they install the company's device management profile. Source code and customer data must only be handled on company laptops.

## What do I do if my TechFlow laptop breaks?
Employees should open a ticket with the IT help desk right away, and TechFlow will repair or replace the laptop within 2 business days. Employees should not attempt to repair company hardware themselves.

## What expenses does TechFlow reimburse?
TechFlow reimburses reasonable business expenses such as travel, client meals, software and approved learning purchases. Personal expenses and alcohol beyond a modest amount are not reimbursed.

## How do I submit an expense report at TechFlow?
TechFlow employees upload receipts and select the category in the expense tool, then submit the report for manager approval. Reports should be filed within 30 days of the expense.

## How quickly are TechFlow expenses reimbursed?
TechFlow reimburses approved expenses in the next payroll after approval, which is either the 15th or the last business day. Reports submitted more than 60 days late may not be paid.

## Does TechFlow provide a company credit card?
TechFlow issues company credit cards to employees who travel regularly or manage vendor purchases, on manager request. Employees must reconcile card purchases in the expense tool every month.

## How do I book business travel at TechFlow?
TechFlow employees book flights and hotels through the company travel portal after getting manager approval. Flights should be booked at least 14 days in advance in economy class.

## What are TechFlow's hotel and meal limits when traveling?
TechFlow allows up to $220 per night for hotels in major cities and $75 per day for meals when traveling. Employees need approval from their manager to exceed those limits.

## Does TechFlow have an on-call rotation?
Yes, TechFlow engineers on production teams join a weekly on-call rotation, usually once every 6 to 8 weeks. The on-call engineer must respond to critical pages within 15 minutes.

## How is on-call work compensated at TechFlow?
TechFlow pays an on-call stipend of $400 per week of rotation, plus compensatory time off for any pages handled between 10:00 PM and 6:00 AM. Compensatory time is taken within 30 days.

## Does TechFlow pay overtime?
TechFlow pays overtime at 1.5 times the hourly rate to non-exempt employees for hours beyond 40 per week. Overtime must be approved in advance by a manager.

## What is the exempt versus non-exempt classification at TechFlow?
Exempt TechFlow employees are salaried and are not eligible for overtime, while non-exempt employees are paid hourly and track their time. The classification is stated in the offer letter.

## What happens during the first week at TechFlow?
TechFlow's first week includes an orientation on Monday, IT setup, meetings with the team and a 1-to-1 with the manager. Each new hire gets a buddy to help with questions.

## What is the TechFlow onboarding buddy program?
Every new TechFlow employee is paired with a buddy from another team for the first 90 days. The buddy answers day-to-day questions and meets the new hire weekly.

## What paperwork do new hires complete at TechFlow?
New TechFlow employees sign the offer letter, confidentiality agreement and equity documents, and complete tax and eligibility forms in the HR portal before their start date. HR sends reminders a week in advance.

## What is the probation period at TechFlow?
TechFlow has a 90-day probation period for new employees during which either side can end employment with a shorter notice. A formal check-in with the manager takes place at day 30, 60 and 90.

## Can I take PTO during probation at TechFlow?
Yes, TechFlow employees can take PTO during probation with manager approval, and sick days are available from the first day. Employees are encouraged to schedule long trips after the probation period.

## What training does TechFlow provide to employees?
TechFlow offers internal workshops, an online course library and mandatory annual training on security and compliance. Employees can also use the $1,500 learning budget for external courses.

## Does TechFlow pay for conferences?
Yes, TechFlow covers conference tickets, travel and lodging for employees whose manager approves the attendance. Conference costs are separate from the $1,500 learning budget when the conference is related to the employee's role.

## Is there mandatory training at TechFlow?
All TechFlow employees complete security awareness, code of conduct and anti-harassment training within 30 days of starting and every year after. Managers receive additional training on people management.

## What does the TechFlow code of conduct say?
TechFlow's code of conduct asks employees to act with honesty, respect and integrity, obey the law and protect company and customer information. Violations can lead to disciplinary action up to termination.

## What is TechFlow's policy on harassment?
TechFlow has zero tolerance for harassment or discrimination of any kind, including bullying, unwanted advances and offensive jokes. This applies in the office, remote work, company events and online chats.

## How do I report harassment at TechFlow?
TechFlow employees can report harassment to their manager, HR or the anonymous ethics hotline. HR acknowledges every report within 2 business days and starts an investigation.

## Does TechFlow protect people who report problems from retaliation?
Yes, TechFlow strictly prohibits retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith or takes part in an investigation. Employees who retaliate face disciplinary action.

## What is TechFlow's whistleblower policy?
TechFlow encourages employees to report suspected fraud, legal violations or safety issues through the ethics hotline, which allows anonymous reports. Reports go directly to the legal team and the audit committee.

## What is TechFlow's conflict of interest policy?
TechFlow employees must disclose any situation where personal interests could affect their work, such as a financial stake in a vendor or a close relative at a competitor. Disclosures are made to HR using the conflict of interest form.

## Can I have a side project or second job at TechFlow?
Yes, TechFlow employees may have side projects if they do not compete with TechFlow, use company resources or interfere with their work. Employees should tell their manager and get written approval for any paid second job.

## Who owns the work I create for TechFlow?
Work that TechFlow employees create as part of their job or with company resources belongs to TechFlow under the invention assignment agreement. Employees may keep side projects that are unrelated and made on their own time.

## Can I contribute to open source at TechFlow?
Yes, TechFlow employees may contribute to open source projects after the legal team confirms the license and that no confidential code is included. Contributions during work hours need manager approval.

## What does TechFlow's confidentiality policy cover?
Confidentiality at TechFlow covers customer data, source code, product plans, financial results and employee personal data. Employees must not share this information outside the company, and the obligation continues after leaving.

## What are TechFlow's password rules?
TechFlow employees must use the company password manager, unique passwords of at least 14 characters and multi-factor authentication on all work accounts. Passwords must never be shared or written on paper.

## What should I do if I lose my TechFlow laptop or phone?
Employees should report a lost or stolen device to the security team immediately, within 1 hour if possible, so it can be locked and wiped remotely. TechFlow laptops use full-disk encryption.

## How do I recognize a phishing email at TechFlow?
TechFlow employees should be careful with unexpected links, urgent requests for payment or credentials and mismatched sender addresses. Suspicious emails should be reported with the Report Phish button, and never clicked.

## What if I clicked a suspicious link at TechFlow?
TechFlow employees who click a suspicious link or enter credentials should disconnect from the network and contact security@techflow.example right away. Reporting quickly is never punished.

## Can I use public Wi-Fi when working for TechFlow?
TechFlow employees may use public Wi-Fi only while connected to the company VPN. Handling customer data on a public network without the VPN is not allowed.

## What is TechFlow's social media policy?
TechFlow employees may use social media freely but must not share confidential information or speak for the company unless authorized. Employees should state that opinions are their own when discussing industry topics.

## What is TechFlow's dress code?
TechFlow has a casual dress code, and employees are free to wear what is comfortable and appropriate for the work. Employees should dress more formally when meeting customers or investors.

## Does TechFlow offer relocation assistance?
TechFlow offers a relocation package of up to $8,000 for approved moves to a company office city, which covers moving costs and temporary housing. If the employee leaves within 12 months of the move, they must repay the package.

## Does TechFlow sponsor work visas?
Yes, TechFlow sponsors work visas such as H-1B for roles where the company decides sponsorship is needed, and pays legal fees. HR starts the process during the offer stage.

## Does TechFlow help with green card sponsorship?
TechFlow starts green card sponsorship for eligible employees after 12 months of employment and a Meets Expectations rating or higher. The company pays the legal fees for the employee's case.

## Who handles immigration questions at TechFlow?
TechFlow employees with visa questions should contact the HR immigration coordinator at immigration@techflow.example. Employees should do so at least 6 months before a visa expires.

## How do I resign from TechFlow?
Employees who resign should tell their manager first, and then send a written resignation letter to HR. TechFlow asks for a notice period of 2 weeks.

## What is TechFlow's notice period?
The notice period at TechFlow is 2 weeks for all employees. Senior roles at director level and above are asked to give 4 weeks.

## Can TechFlow employees be asked to leave immediately after resigning?
TechFlow may choose to end an employee's last day earlier than the notice period for security reasons, and in that case pays the employee through the full notice period. This is rare and decided by HR.

## What is the TechFlow offboarding process?
When an employee leaves, TechFlow's offboarding includes a knowledge transfer plan, return of the laptop and badge, access removal on the last day and an exit interview with HR. HR provides a checklist a week before the last day.

## What final pay do TechFlow employees receive?
TechFlow pays the final salary through the last day worked, plus any approved expenses, in the final paycheck, issued on the last day where required by law. Unused PTO is not paid out because PTO is unlimited.

## Can I keep my laptop or phone number after leaving TechFlow?
No, TechFlow employees must return the company laptop and equipment within 5 business days after leaving, and TechFlow ships a prepaid box for it. Employees can keep a personal phone number they used for work.

## What happens to my health insurance after I leave TechFlow?
TechFlow health coverage ends on the last day of the month in which employment ends. Former employees can continue coverage through COBRA at their own cost for up to 18 months.

## What happens to my 401(k) after I leave TechFlow?
Former TechFlow employees keep their full 401(k) balance, including the vested company match, and can leave it in the plan, roll it into an IRA or transfer it to a new employer's plan. HR provides rollover instructions.

## What severance does TechFlow offer in a layoff?
In a layoff, TechFlow offers 2 weeks of base pay for each year of service, with a minimum of 8 weeks and a maximum of 26 weeks. Severance requires signing a standard separation agreement.

## What else does TechFlow provide to employees affected by layoffs?
Employees affected by layoffs receive 3 months of company-paid health insurance, outplacement services and an extended equity exercise window of 12 months. TechFlow gives at least 60 days of notice where required by law.

## Can I transfer to another team at TechFlow?
Yes, TechFlow employees may apply for internal transfers after 12 months in their current role, or sooner with manager approval. Open roles are posted on the internal jobs board.

## How does the internal transfer process work at TechFlow?
Employees apply on the internal jobs board, interview with the hiring team and then discuss the move with their current manager. The transfer typically takes effect within 4 weeks of acceptance.

## Does my pay change if I transfer teams at TechFlow?
A lateral transfer at TechFlow keeps the same salary and equity, while a move to a higher level follows the promotion process. Pay is adjusted if the new role has a different location band.

## Who should I contact in HR at TechFlow?
TechFlow employees can reach HR at hr@techflow.example or through the Ask HR channel in chat. The HR team answers within 1 business day.

## Where can I find TechFlow policies and forms?
All TechFlow policies, forms and benefit guides are in the HR portal under the Library section. Employees can also search the portal or ask the HR chat assistant.

## How can employees give feedback or suggestions at TechFlow?
TechFlow runs an anonymous employee survey every quarter and holds a monthly all-hands with open Q and A. Employees can also share ideas directly through the Ask HR channel.
